2. What data we process

The forms on this website (request to install Tuko and the tuko AI waitlist) collect, because you type them in:

  • Full name

  • Your store's website

If you accept analytics cookies, visits are also measured with Google Analytics 4 (section 9).

3. What we use it for

  • Replying to your request and, if you ask, helping you install Tuko or giving you a demo.
  • Letting you know when tuko AI is available, if you join its waitlist.

We do not profile you or make automated decisions with your data. We will only send you marketing communications if you expressly agree.

4. Legal basis

Handling what you ask for when you submit a form: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The tuko AI waitlist: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Analytics: your consent to cookies (section 9).

5. Who else accesses the data

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. These providers process it on our behalf:

  • Netlify, Inc. (USA): website hosting and form handling.
  • Google Ireland Ltd.: Google Analytics 4, only if you accept analytics cookies.

Both may process data in the USA. That transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

Form data: 12 months from our last contact, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or it becomes a business relationship (then, as long as the law requires). Waitlist data: until tuko AI is available or you unsubscribe.

7. Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction or portability of your data, and withdraw your consent, by writing to joan@tukoteam.com.

If you think we have not handled your request properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration.

9. Cookies and analytics

Only if you accept the cookie notice do we load Google Analytics 4 (ID G-W751M44D2X), a third-party service. Without your consent no analytics cookie is set.

  • Provider: Google Ireland Ltd.
  • Purpose: pseudonymised, aggregated measurement of how the website is used (page views, traffic sources).
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 22(2) of the Spanish LSSI).
  • Cookies: _ga and _ga_<ID>, lasting about 2 years.
  • International transfer: Google LLC (USA), covered by the Data Privacy Framework.
  • Your choice: stored in your browser (localStorage, key tuko_cookie_consent); we ask again after 13 months.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with this button or the «Cookies» link in the footer. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
